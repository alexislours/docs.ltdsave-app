The Player tab edits everything in `Player.sav`, that's the island-wide state, not the Miis themselves. Your name, your island, how much money you have, what you've unlocked, what's sitting in your stores, which fountain wishes you've cleared, and the UGC word list the game uses for dialogue all live here.

Once `Player.sav` is loaded, the tab splits into sub-tabs across the top: **Profile**, **Foods**, **Clothes**, **Clothing sets**, **Treasures**, **Interiors**, **Buildings**, **Wishes**, **UGC**, and **Advanced**.

<Image src={playerSubtabs} alt="Player tab with the sub-tab bar" />

## Download Player.sav

The orange **Download Player.sav** button at the top of the tab writes a patched copy of your save to your Downloads folder. The editor never overwrites the file on disk, you do that yourself by putting the downloaded file back where you found it.

The button stays greyed out until you've made an edit, and lights up the moment something changes. You can keep editing and re-download as many times as you want, each download is the full current state.

## Profile

The **Profile** sub-tab groups the small handful of identity and global progress fields.

<Image src={playerProfile} alt="Profile sub-tab" />

### Identity

- **Player name** and **Island** are the two names everyone sees in-game. Each one has a matching **Pronounced** field, the phonetic spelling the game uses for voice playback. Leave the pronounced fields alone if the regular name already reads correctly.
- **Skin tone** picks the hand color used on the player's hand.
- **Island size** sets how much of the island is available to you.

### Stats

- **Money**, max is `999,999.99`. The dropdown next to it picks the **currency** used in display.
- **Birthday** uses three separate day/month/year leaves under the hood, the date picker keeps them in sync.
- **Play time** is stored in seconds. The field shows the raw count, with a human-readable `h m` summary next to it so you can sanity-check what you typed.
- **Boots**, how many times you booted the game.

### Fountain

- **Fountain level** is your current fountain rank, the gate for most wish unlocks.
- **Wishes** is the number of wishes available.
- **Wishes come true** is the cumulative counter of wishes that have already been granted, several late-game unlocks gate on this number rather than on fountain level.

These three are also what the **Wishes** sub-tab will offer to bump for you when you unlock a gated wish (see below).

## Inventory sub-tabs

**Foods**, **Clothes**, **Clothing sets**, **Treasures**, **Interiors**, and **Buildings** all share the same shape. They list every item the save tracks, with two controls per row:

- **State** is the unlock status: **Locked**, **New**, **Arrived**, **Opened**, or **Obtained**. **Obtained** is the one that makes an item show up as unlocked in your store or collection.
- **OwnNum** (the **+/-** stepper) is how many you have in inventory.

<Image src={playerFoods} alt="Foods sub-tab" />

The search box at the top filters by name, both translated and internal. Type a few characters to narrow the list down, the row count below the heading updates as you type.

Some sub-tabs have an extra level of grouping. **Clothes** and **Clothing sets** roll up the color variants of one garment into one row, click the row to expand and edit a single color. **Interiors** does the same for style variants.

### Bulk edit

Each sub-tab has a **Bulk edit (advanced)** drawer hidden behind a warning bar at the top of the list. Open it to set every visible row to the same state, the same quantity, or both. The bulk operation applies to whatever the current search filter shows, so type a search first to scope what you're about to overwrite.

<Aside type="caution">
  Bulk edit cannot be undone except by reloading the original save. Make a backup before using it.
</Aside>

## Wishes

The **Wishes** sub-tab edits the wishes available at the fountain. Each row is one wish with a checkbox, ticking the box marks the wish as obtained. Unticking removes it.

<Image src={playerWishes} alt="Wishes sub-tab" />

Each wish carries one or more **gates** that gate when the game considers it cleared:

- **Lv. N** is the minimum fountain level required.
- **After N wishes** is the cumulative number of wish granted required.

Gates show in green when your current stats meet them and red when they don't.

If you tick a wish whose level or count gate you don't meet, the editor pops a **Bump stats to satisfy unlock?** dialog. Confirm to raise your fountain level and/or wishes-come-true counter to exactly the threshold, skip to leave them as-is. Skipping is fine if you plan to bump those stats from the Profile tab yourself.

**Unlock visible** and **Reset visible** at the bottom apply to whatever the current search and category filter shows. Same warning as bulk inventory edits, both buttons confirm before running.

The **UGC** sub-tab edits the custom word list.

## Advanced

The **Advanced** sub-tab is the raw entry browser for `Player.sav`. Every leaf in the file is listed and editable here, including the ones the curated sub-tabs don't expose.

<Aside type="caution">
  Always back up your `Player.sav` before downloading the edited copy over the original. A bad value
  in here is the kind of thing the game won't load at all, and there's no in-game recovery.
</Aside>
