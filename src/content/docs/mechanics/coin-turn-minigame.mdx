**Coin Spin** (internal name `CoinTurn`) is a one-on-one minigame: a Mii spins a coin, you call **Heads or Tails** before it settles, and a correct call wins a prize.

## The odds

Each spin, the game does a weighted roll to decide how to bias the coin, then applies that as a center-of-mass offset and physically spins it.

| Roll              | Coin behaviour                                   | Rate                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| `PlusOffsetRate`  | Center of mass shifted one way → lands **Heads** | **47.5%** (`0.475`) |
| `MinusOffsetRate` | Shifted the other way → lands **Tails**          | **47.5%** (`0.475`) |
| `ZeroOffsetRate`  | Balanced coin → stops on its **edge**            | **5%** (`0.05`)     |

This roll is independent of your call. The **edge landing counts as an automatic win**, so whichever side you pick your effective win chance is:

> 47.5% (your side) + 5% (edge) = **52.5%**

So, the game is very slightly in your favor.

## Outcomes

| Result                          | Effect        |
| ------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Your call matches               | Win.          |
| Your call is wrong              | No prize.     |
| Coin lands on its **edge** (5%) | Automatic win |
