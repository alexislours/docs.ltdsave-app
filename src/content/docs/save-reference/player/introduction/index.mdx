## Fields

### `EmotionBallAtGraduate`

<LeafMeta name="EmotionBallAtGraduate" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

Total accumulation of emotion balls at the time when the player graduated early stage.

The calculations are done by taking these three parameters:

1. The players currently held emotion balls ([`Player.EmotionBall`](../player/#emotionball)).
2. Amount of emotion balls inserted into the fountain ([`Liberation.FountainEmotionBall`](../liberation/#fountainemotionball)).
3. The fountain level ([`Liberation.FountainLevel`](../liberation/#fountainlevel)).

It then calculates how many emotion balls have been spent leveling up the fountain and sums it with the two other parameters. Level costs can be found at `Parameter/LiberationSystemParam/System.game__LiberationSystemParam.bgyml`.

<Bounds>
  <Fragment slot="ingame">
    Any `UInt` value; capped at `4,294,967,295`. The game calculates this by summing the costs of
    every Fountain Level (using Level 10's cost for all levels above 10). Each level cost is forced
    to a minimum of `1`.
  </Fragment>
</Bounds>
