### `BuyMarketSale`

<LeafMeta name="BuyMarketSale" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

### `CoinGold`

<LeafMeta name="CoinGold" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

Counts the **gold coins** goods the player received from Miis during the current day. A Mii hands over a gold coin as a level-up "payback" gift when its happiness reaches level 21.

### `CoinSilver`

<LeafMeta name="CoinSilver" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

Counts the **silver coins** goods the player acquired during the current day. A Mii hands over a silver coin as a level-up "payback" gift when its happiness reaches the highest level 20.
