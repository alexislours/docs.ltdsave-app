These leaves control the state of interior styles owned by the player.

## Shape

Every entry under `Player.InteriorRoomStyleInfo` has the same shape. The fields are documented once below, per sub-key hashes are in the **Instances** table.

### `FirstObtainedSec`

<LeafMeta name="FirstObtainedSec" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

POSIX timestamp in seconds (`nn::time::PosixTime`) recorded the very first time the player ever acquires this room style.

<Bounds>
  <Fragment slot="ingame">
    `0` if the room style has never been acquired, otherwise a POSIX timestamp at or after the
    game's release.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="accepted">any `UInt64` value.</Fragment>
</Bounds>

### `InformedNewRelease`

<LeafMeta name="InformedNewRelease" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

Whether the shop has already shown the "new arrival" weekly announcement for this specific room-style variant.

Only styles flagged `IsWeeklyItem` in `romfs/RSDB/RoomStyleInteriorShopSetting.Product.[ver].rstbl.byml.yml` can ever become `true` in normal gameplay.

### `IsNewlyOwned`

<LeafMeta name="IsNewlyOwned" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

This boolean controls whether the room style has recently been acquired by the player. This is what drives the "NEW!" badge on each variant tile in the reform / room-style picker.

Opening the room-style picker on that variant clears this boolean.

<ValueDescriptions name="IsNewlyOwned" leaves={frontmatter.leaves}>
  <Fragment slot="false">
    The room style hasn't been acquired, or it has been acquired and the player has opened the
    room-style picker on it since.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="true">
    The player hasn't opened the room-style picker on it since obtaining the room style.
  </Fragment>
</ValueDescriptions>

### `KeyHash`

<LeafMeta name="KeyHash" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

Inline counterpart of the array-form [`Player.BuildingInfo2.KeyHash`](../building-info2/#keyhash) and [`Player.FloorInfo.KeyHash`](../floor-info/#keyhash). The field is unused for room-styles.

<Bounds>
  <Fragment slot="ingame">always `0`.</Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="accepted">any `UInt32` value.</Fragment>
</Bounds>

### `LastObtainedSec`

<LeafMeta name="LastObtainedSec" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

POSIX timestamp in seconds (`nn::time::PosixTime`) recorded when:

- The `State` goes from `Lock` → `Obtained`.
- The room style is obtained again.

`0` if the room style isn't currently held as `Obtained`.

<Bounds>
  <Fragment slot="ingame">
    `0` if the room style isn't currently held as `Obtained`, otherwise a POSIX timestamp at or
    after the game's release.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="accepted">any `UInt64` value.</Fragment>
</Bounds>

### `OwnNum`

<LeafMeta name="OwnNum" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

The number of copies of this room-style variant the player currently holds. Decremented by 1 each time the variant is applied to a Mii's room.

While the variant has never been obtained by the player it stays at the schema default `-2`, paired with `State = Lock`. Changed to a non-negative count once the variant becomes obtainable.

<Bounds>
  <Fragment slot="ingame">
    `-2` while the variant is `Locked`, otherwise a non-negative count of held copies.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="accepted">any `Int` value.</Fragment>
</Bounds>

### `State`

<LeafMeta name="State" leaves={frontmatter.leaves} />

The lifecycle state of the room-style variant.

<ValueDescriptions name="State" leaves={frontmatter.leaves}>
  <Fragment slot="Lock">
    The room-style variant isn't unlocked or in the shop yet. Paired with `OwnNum = -2`.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="New">Unused by room-styles but a valid value.</Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="Arrived">Unused by room-styles but a valid value.</Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="Opened">
    The shop has revealed the variant to the player but not purchased yet. Paired with `OwnNum = 0`.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="Obtained">
    The player has obtained this variant at least once. Sticky: even if `OwnNum` goes back to `0`,
    does not demote it back to `Opened`.
  </Fragment>
</ValueDescriptions>
